Third-party material shipped with the polyemesis Stream Deck plugin

Simple Icons (https://simpleicons.org): the YouTube, Twitch, Kick, Facebook and X marks drawn on
destination keys (src/keyImage.ts, bundled into bin/plugin.js). The SVG paths are copied verbatim
from the published icons, the same paths as ui/src/components/platformGlyphs.tsx in the polyemesis
repository. Source: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons

The marks are drawn in one colour, the key state's title colour, and are used only to identify the
service a destination streams to. They remain the trade marks of their respective owners; their use
here does not imply endorsement by, or affiliation with, those owners.
