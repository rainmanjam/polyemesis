Third-party data in the polyemesis automod pack

The pack (en-v1.json) and the skeleton table (skeleton_table.go) are derived
from the works below. The pack is a modified derivative; it is not the upstream
lists and is not endorsed by their authors.

1. dsojevic/profanity-list (MIT)
   https://github.com/dsojevic/profanity-list
   Copyright (c) 2021 David Sojevic
   Commit c27924319aa9bd6f917e3782b4f4b6604a50b652. Licence: LICENSE-dsojevic-profanity-list.txt
   Seeds the hate terms, the profanity terms and the exception lists.

2. List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words, English (CC-BY-4.0)
   https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words
   Credit: LDNOOBW contributors.
   Commit 5faf2ba42d7b1c0977169ec3611df25a3c08eb13.
   Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International,
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ (full text: LICENSE-ldnoobw-CC-BY-4.0.txt)
   Seeds the optional profanity terms.
   CHANGES MADE: the list was modified. Entries were filtered (terms that are
   too short, contain digits that are not letter substitutes, or have more than
   four words were removed), lowercased, normalised to the pack form (NFKC,
   look-alike characters folded to ASCII, repeated letters collapsed to two),
   de-duplicated against the other source, tagged, and merged with the other
   list. No endorsement by the LDNOOBW contributors is implied.

3. Unicode Security Mechanisms, confusables.txt, version 17.0.0
   https://www.unicode.org/Public/17.0.0/security/confusables.txt
   Copyright (c) 1991-2026 Unicode, Inc. Licence: Unicode License v3,
   LICENSE-Unicode-3.0.txt. Filtered to single characters that look like an
   ASCII letter or digit, and lowercased.
